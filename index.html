🚀 Our Flagship Product: AI-Powered Document Parser (SaaS / API)

Automated data extraction from PDFs, scans, and invoices into structured JSON formats leveraging state-of-the-art Vision LLMs.

The Problem: Modern B2B enterprises waste hundreds of hours daily on manual data entry from unstructured formats (PDFs, images, or photos) into internal ERP, CRM, or accounting systems.

Our Solution: An API-first SaaS platform that completely automates document processing. Built on top of the robust Symfony / Laravel ecosystem for secure backend infrastructure, and powered by a high-performance asynchronous queue architecture for rapid document processing, our engine leverages Vision LLMs with Structured Outputs functionality to instantly return a validated, structured JSON array ready for instant integration.
